Internal medicine is the medical specialty dealing with the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of adult diseases. As an internist, I have a holistic view of the patient and coordinate care when multiple specialists are required.

## Areas of Practice

- **Systemic diseases**: Lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, vasculitis
- **Complex infectious diseases**: Endocarditis, osteomyelitis, sepsis
- **Perioperative medicine**: Pre-surgical evaluation and optimization
- **Complex single-organ pathology**: Advanced heart failure, severe COPD, chronic kidney disease

## Diagnostic Approach

I use an evidence-based clinical approach, integrating detailed history, thorough physical examination, and targeted complementary tests to reach accurate diagnoses efficiently.
