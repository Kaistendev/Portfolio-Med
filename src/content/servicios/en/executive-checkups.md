Executive checkups are designed for high-responsibility professionals seeking a comprehensive health evaluation in a single day.

## Includes

- **Complete medical history**: Personal, family, toxic habits, occupational
- **Thorough physical examination**: Cardiovascular, respiratory, abdominal, neurological, dermatological
- **Complete blood work**: CBC, biochemistry, lipid profile, glucose, thyroid, hepatic, renal function, tumor markers
- **Imaging**: Chest X-ray, abdominal ultrasound, echocardiogram
- **Functional tests**: Spirometry, stress test (ergometry)
- **Age/sex-specific screening**: Colonoscopy, mammography, PSA, DEXA scan

## Outcome

Detailed report with findings, cardiovascular risk stratification, and personalized lifestyle and follow-up recommendations.
