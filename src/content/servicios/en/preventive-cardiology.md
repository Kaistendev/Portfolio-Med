Preventive cardiology focuses on identifying and modifying cardiovascular risk factors before clinical disease develops.

## Services

- **Cardiovascular risk calculation**: SCORE, Framingham, REGICOR scales
- **Hypertension control**: Ambulatory monitoring (ABPM), medication adjustment
- **Dyslipidemia management**: Statins, ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors, nutrition
- **Arterial disease screening**: Carotid ultrasound, ankle-brachial index
- **Cardiac rehabilitation**: Supervised program post-MI/surgery

## Primary and secondary prevention

Primary prevention in asymptomatic patients with risk factors. Secondary prevention in patients with established cardiovascular disease.
