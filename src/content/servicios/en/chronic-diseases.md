Chronic disease management requires continuous follow-up, therapeutic adjustment, and patient education to prevent complications and maintain quality of life.

## Main Conditions

- **Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus**: Glycemic control, prevention of micro/macrovascular complications, technology (flash sensor, insulin pump)
- **Hypertension**: Risk stratification, stepped treatment, ambulatory monitoring (ABPM)
- **Dyslipidemia**: LDL targets by cardiovascular risk, novel therapies (PCSK9 inhibitors, bempedoic acid)
- **COPD**: GOLD therapeutic ladder, pulmonary rehabilitation, exacerbation management
- **Heart Failure**: Quadruple therapy (ACEi/ARB/ARNI, beta-blocker, MRA, SGLT2i)

## Structured Follow-up

- Scheduled visits every 3-6 months
- Control labs (HbA1c, lipid profile, renal function, electrolytes)
- Adherence and adverse effects review
- Coordination with primary care nursing and specialists (nephrology, cardiology, endocrinology)
