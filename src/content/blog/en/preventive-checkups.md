Prevention is the foundation of good health. Periodic check-ups allow early detection of risk factors and diseases when they are most treatable.

## What does an executive check-up include?

- **Complete medical history**: Personal and family history, lifestyle habits
- **Physical examination**: Vital signs, system-by-system examination
- **Comprehensive blood work**: CBC, metabolic panel, lipid profile, glucose, liver/kidney function
- **Imaging**: Chest X-ray, abdominal ultrasound, echocardiogram
- **Functional tests**: Spirometry, stress test, fundoscopy

## Recommended frequency

- **30-40 years**: Every 2-3 years if no risk factors
- **40-50 years**: Every 1-2 years
- **>50 years**: Annually

The investment in prevention is always lower than the cost of treating advanced disease.
